Why Fine-tune RoBERTa, DeBERTa, and DistilBERT?

DistilBERT

A distilled version of BERT that retains 97% of its performance while being 40% smaller and 60% faster. Ideal for low-latency batch processing and resource-constrained environments.

RoBERTa

Robustly Optimized BERT Approach. Re-trains BERT with a larger batch size, more data, and dynamic masking. Delivers strong accuracy, perfect for generating detail-rich BI insights.

DeBERTa

Decoding-enhanced BERT with disentangled attention. Re-evaluates relative positions of words dynamically. Often achieves the highest accuracy, providing enterprise-grade sentiment insights.

Why Fine-tune RoBERTa, DeBERTa, and DistilBERT?

DistilBERT

A distilled version of BERT that retains 97% of its performance while being 40% smaller and 60% faster. Achieves ~92% accuracy, making it ideal for low-latency batch processing.

RoBERTa

Robustly Optimized BERT Approach. Re-trains BERT with a larger batch size, more data, and dynamic masking. Achieves ~94% accuracy, perfect for generating detail-rich BI insights.

DeBERTa

Decoding-enhanced BERT with disentangled attention. Re-evaluates relative positions of words dynamically. Reaches the highest accuracy (~94.6%), providing enterprise-grade sentiment insights.
